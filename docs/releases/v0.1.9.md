# nanoMuse 0.1.9 · Portrait

The face, Muse's way. Changing the avatar is now a sentence in the chat — *change your avatar to a corgi*, with a reference picture attached if you like. The agent answers with four takes in a card; tap one, or say "the second one", and it announces its new look while the poses are drawn behind the scenes. Tap the face and you are on the agent's own page. The name under the face is re-measured against Muse's, and the face comes in five sizes.

## Added

- **Change the avatar in the chat.** Say it in Chinese or English — 把虚拟形象换成小狗, *change your avatar to a corgi*, *new avatar: a fox in a scarf* — and the request never reaches your chat model: four candidates are drawn by your image model into a 2×2 card. Attach a picture first and the takes are drawn from it. Pick by tapping, or by typing (第二个, *2*, *the second one*, 左上, *last*); *Again* redraws, *Keep current* stops. The chosen face is on at once, the agent says so, the four takes stay in the transcript with your pick marked, the poses land in the header as they finish, and one line goes into its memory.
- **Muse's house style.** The default look is a soft-matte 3D toy render — full body, facing you, centred on white — and the four takes vary colouring, breed and outfit. The poses are Muse's fixed set: headphones and a laptop while working, a crystal ball while waiting for you, a five-pointed star when pleased, a sweat drop when something failed.
- **Share it.** When the new look lands, a card offers to share it: five pastel cards with the face, a speech bubble and the nanoMuse wordmark, sent through the system share sheet. Also from the agent's page.
- **The agent's page.** Tap the face or the name on any home tab: ×, share, the face with a pen (→ *Change avatar*, *Edit name*, *Avatar studio*), the name, *online*, and four panes — today's and yesterday's activity (what you asked, what it used, when; tap to open), what you have approved as *Always*, its daily routines, and the SOUL and Memory cards. *Change avatar* brings you back to the chat with "Change your avatar to " already typed.
- **Avatar size.** *Settings → Appearance → Avatar size*: Small, Medium, Large, Extra large, Hidden. The header follows as you tap.

## Changed

- **The name under the face** is measured against Muse's: a white pill with soft corners, the name in 14sp regular (it was heavier and larger), and the status — *Working…*, *Needs approval*, *Generating options* — as a small grey second line inside the pill.
- The welcome screen can no longer reappear after you leave an empty main chat for another page and come back.
- versionCode 10; installs over 0.1.8 without losing data.

## Fixed

- Since 0.1.6, one of the four candidate tiles in the avatar studio could stay loading forever when two finished at the same moment.

## Not in this version

- The animated states (head shake, crystal ball, star, laptop) are still the existing pose cross-fades; a video model behind them is being tried out.
- The bundled default face is still the red panda; a neutral Muse-like character is in the works.

## Known issues

- arm64 only; for an x86 emulator, build a test APK yourself with `-Pnm.abi=x86_64`.
- Drawing needs an image model on one of your providers (OpenAI-compatible `/images/generations`, or Alibaba Cloud Model Studio's qwen-image); without one the agent says so and points at the setting.

## Install

Download `nanoMuse-0.1.9-arm64.apk` (Android 8.0 or newer, arm64) and install it over the previous version — same signing key, your data is kept. Verify: `sha256sum -c nanoMuse-0.1.9-arm64.apk.sha256`.

换脸，照 Muse 的路。换形象现在是对话里的一句话——「把虚拟形象换成小狗」，愿意的话可以先附一张参考图。它回你四张候选，点一张或者说「第二个」，它宣布新形象，姿势在后台接着画。点脸就进它自己的资料页。脸下面的名字牌按 Muse 的重新测过，脸有五档大小。

### 新增

- **对话里换形象。** 中英文都认——「把虚拟形象换成小狗」「change your avatar to a corgi」「换个形象：戴围巾的狐狸」——这句话不会送到你的对话模型：由你的图像模型画四张候选，排成 2×2 的卡。先附一张图，四张就照着图画。点选，或者打字选（第二个、2、the second one、左上、最后一个）；「再来四张」重画，「保持现在的」放弃。选中的脸立刻换上，它会说一声，四张候选留在记录里并标出你选的那张，姿势画好一张就在页头换一张，记忆里记一行。
- **Muse 的固定风格。** 默认是柔哑光的 3D 玩具渲染——全身、正面、白底居中——四张在配色、品种和装扮上各不相同。姿势是 Muse 那几套：戴耳机敲笔记本（工作中）、玩水晶球（等你）、玩五角星（开心）、冒汗（出错）。
- **分享。** 新形象换好后弹一张卡邀请你分享：五张马卡龙色的卡片，脸、一句对话气泡、nanoMuse 字标，走系统分享面板。资料页也能分享。
- **它的资料页。** 在任何首页 tab 点脸或名字：×、分享、带🖊️的脸（→ 更换虚拟形象 / 编辑名称 / 形象工作室）、名字、「在线」，四个面板——今天和昨天的动态（你问了什么、它用了什么、几点；点开可回到那段对话）、你批准为「总是允许」的事、每日例程、SOUL 和记忆卡。「更换虚拟形象」把你带回对话，输入框里已经写好「把虚拟形象改成」。
- **形象大小。** 设置 → 外观 → 形象大小：小 / 中 / 大 / 超大 / 隐藏，页头即时跟着变。

### 变更

- **脸下面的名字牌**按 Muse 的界面测过：白色圆角小牌，名字 14sp 常规（之前更粗更大），状态——「工作中…」「需要审批」「正在生成候选」——作为一行小的灰字放进牌子里。
- 主对话还是空的时候去了别的页面再回来，欢迎页不会再冒出来。
- versionCode 10；覆盖安装 0.1.8 不丢数据。

### 修复

- 0.1.6 起，形象工作室的四张候选里如果两张同时画完，可能有一张一直转圈。

### 这版没有

- 动态状态（摇头、水晶球、五角星、敲电脑）仍是现有姿势的淡入淡出；背后接视频模型还在试。
- 内置默认脸仍是小熊猫；中性的 Muse 风格角色在做。

### 已知问题

- 只有 arm64 包；x86 模拟器请自行用 `-Pnm.abi=x86_64` 打测试包。
- 画图需要某个服务商上有图像模型（OpenAI 兼容的 `/images/generations`，或阿里云百炼的 qwen-image）；没有的话它会说明并指向设置。

### 安装

下载 `nanoMuse-0.1.9-arm64.apk`（Android 8.0+，arm64），覆盖安装，同一把签名，数据保留；校验：`sha256sum -c nanoMuse-0.1.9-arm64.apk.sha256`。
