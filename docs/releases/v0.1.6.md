# nanoMuse 0.1.6 · Avatar

The agent gets a face of your choosing. Describe it in a sentence, your own image model draws four, you pick one, and the app poses it for every state the agent can be in — then the face on the disc breathes, bobs, tilts, pops and shakes with what the agent is doing, the way Muse's does. Around it, the small motions that make an app feel finished: pages that cross-fade, feed cards that settle in, a heart that pops.

## Added

- **Avatar page** (Settings → Avatar, or tap the face on any header). Muse's layout: the face on its disc with the agent's name and what it is doing right now, the preview cycling through the moods; *Describe a new face* — one sentence, a row of style chips (flat, 3D clay, watercolour, pixel, line art, sticker) and a blue *Draw four*; *Pick one* — a 2×2 grid of candidates with a shimmer while they draw, a check on the chosen one, and *Use this one*. Failed tiles say why and retry on tap. The ⋯ menu holds *Name and style* (the soul editor), *Image model*, *Redraw the moods* and *Back to the red panda*.
- **Bring your own image model.** Generation goes through the providers you already configured — no new key: any enabled OpenAI-compatible, OpenRouter or xAI instance, with the catalogue's image models offered as chips and a sensible default per host (`qwen-image-3.0` on Alibaba Bailian / DashScope, `gpt-image-1` on OpenAI, `grok-2-image` on xAI, `google/gemini-2.5-flash-image` on OpenRouter). Candidates use OpenMinis' `images/generations`; the four moods are image *edits* of the picture you chose — *working* wears headphones at a laptop, *waiting* looks up with a question mark, *happy* hugs a star, *error* has a sweat drop — through `images/edits` on OpenAI-style hosts and DashScope's native `qwen-image-edit-max` on Alibaba hosts, so the character stays the same. Two calls run at a time; rate limits are retried twice with a growing pause.
- **The face moves.** The avatar cross-fades between moods (260 ms) and animates them: a slow breath at rest (2.6 s, +1.8 %), a quicker breath and a 2.5 dp bob while working, a ±4° tilt while it waits for you, a spring pop when a turn ends well, a 420 ms shake when it failed. A custom face fills the whole disc; the built-in red panda keeps its inset. The afterglow only reads the run that just ended — a plain reply after an old failure smiles — and a run you stopped ends quietly.
- **Pictures stay on the phone**: `minis-global/nanomuse/avatar/{base,working,waiting,happy,error}.png` (512 px), `candidates/0–3.png` and `avatar.json` (prompt, style, model, time). Candidates survive restarts until you draw again; *Back to the red panda* removes the face and its moods but keeps the candidates.
- **Motion elsewhere**: home tabs cross-fade (160 ms in, 120 ms out; the chat underneath fades a touch slower so the switch reads as one), new feed posts settle in and deleted ones fade out, the like heart pops with a spring and its colour eases.

## Changed

- The header's face opens the avatar page; the name pill still opens the soul settings (name, style).
- Deep link `minis://settings/avatar` (also `/face`).
- versionCode 7; installs over 0.1.5 without losing data.

## Kept, on purpose

- The built-in red panda is still the default and one tap away; the launcher icon is untouched. Provider instances, keys and OAuth sign-ins are OpenMinis' own — the avatar page only reads what you configured there.

## Known issues

- Image models differ a lot: how stable the candidates are, and whether the four moods keep the same character, depends on the model. Tested with Alibaba Bailian's `qwen-image-3.0` + `qwen-image-edit-max`; OpenAI's `gpt-image-1` takes the same `images/edits` path but was not exercised in this build.
- One face costs five image calls (the chosen picture plus four moods), billed by your provider.
- arm64 only; for an x86 emulator, build a test APK yourself with `-Pnm.abi=x86_64`.

## Install

Download `nanoMuse-0.1.6-arm64.apk` (Android 8.0 or newer, arm64) and install it over the previous version — same signing key, your data is kept. Verify: `sha256sum -c nanoMuse-0.1.6-arm64.apk.sha256`.

它有了一张你挑的脸。一句话描述，用你自己的图像模型画四张，选一张，App 再给它摆出每种工作状态的姿势——然后圆盘上的脸会跟着它在做的事呼吸、点头、歪头、跳一下、抖一下，和 Muse 的一样。周围是让一个 App 显得完整的那些小动作：页面淡入淡出，动态卡片落进来，红心会跳。

### 新增

- **形象页**（设置 → 形象；或点任何页头上的脸）。Muse 的布局：圆盘上的脸、名字、它此刻在做什么，预览会轮流展示几种状态；「描述一张新脸」——一句话、一排风格（扁平 / 3D 黏土 / 水彩 / 像素 / 线稿 / 贴纸）和蓝色的「画四张」；「选一张」——2×2 的候选格，画的时候有微光，选中的打勾，然后「就用这张」。失败的格子会写明原因，点一下重试。⋯ 菜单里有「名字与风格」（人格编辑器）、「图像模型」、「重画几种状态」、「换回小熊猫」。
- **用你自己的图像模型。** 生成走你已经配好的 provider——不用新填 key：任何启用的 OpenAI 兼容 / OpenRouter / xAI 实例，模型目录里的图像模型做成快捷标签，每个域名有合理的默认（阿里云百炼 / DashScope 用 `qwen-image-3.0`，OpenAI 用 `gpt-image-1`，xAI 用 `grok-2-image`，OpenRouter 用 `google/gemini-2.5-flash-image`）。候选图走 OpenMinis 的 `images/generations`；四种状态是对你选中那张的**图像编辑**——工作时戴耳机敲笔记本、等你时抬头带问号、开心时抱星星、出错时冒汗——OpenAI 风格的域名走 `images/edits`，阿里域名走 DashScope 原生的 `qwen-image-edit-max`，所以角色不会变。同时最多两路请求，限流自动重试两次。
- **脸会动。** 头像在状态之间交叉淡入（260 ms），并给每种状态配动作：休息时慢呼吸（2.6 s，+1.8 %），工作时呼吸加快并上下 2.5 dp 点头，等你时 ±4° 歪头，一轮顺利结束时弹一下，失败时 420 ms 的抖动。自定义的脸铺满整个圆盘，内置小熊猫保留内边距。结束后的表情只看刚结束这一轮的结果——上一轮失败过、这一轮只是普通回复，也会笑；你自己按停的那一轮安静结束。
- **图片留在手机上**：`minis-global/nanomuse/avatar/{base,working,waiting,happy,error}.png`（512 px）、`candidates/0–3.png` 和 `avatar.json`（描述、风格、模型、时间）。候选图在重启后仍在，直到你再画一次；「换回小熊猫」删除脸和状态图，保留候选。
- **别处的动效**：首页四个页签交叉淡入淡出（进 160 ms、出 120 ms，底下的聊天稍慢一点淡出，看起来是一次切换），新的动态帖子落进来、删除的淡出，点赞的红心带弹簧跳一下、颜色渐变。

### 变更

- 页头的脸打开形象页；名字胶囊照旧打开人格设置（名字、风格）。
- 深链 `minis://settings/avatar`（也认 `/face`）。
- versionCode 7；覆盖安装 0.1.5 不丢数据。

### 有意保留

- 内置小熊猫仍是默认，一键换回；启动图标没动。Provider 实例、key 和 OAuth 都是 OpenMinis 自己的——形象页只读你在那里配好的东西。

### 已知问题

- 图像模型的能力差别很大：候选图的稳定性、四种状态是否保持同一角色，取决于模型。测试用的是百炼的 `qwen-image-3.0` + `qwen-image-edit-max`；OpenAI 的 `gpt-image-1` 走同样的 `images/edits` 路径，但没有在本版本里实测。
- 一张脸要花五次图像调用（一张选中的 + 四种状态），按你的 provider 计费。
- 只有 arm64 包；x86 模拟器请自行用 `-Pnm.abi=x86_64` 打测试包。
