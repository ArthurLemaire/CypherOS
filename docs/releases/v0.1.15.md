# nanoMuse 0.1.15 · Stage

What the hands do is now something to watch, and the capsule no longer gets in their way.

## Added

- **The stage.** While the hands work, a glow breathes along the edges of the screen — blue while they work, amber while they wait for you. Where the model is about to tap, a red dashed ring turns with a dot at its centre and the action's name beside it — *Tap “Search”*, *Hold*, *Double-tap* — a moment before the finger lands, then a ripple as it lands. A long press fills a second ring for as long as it is held; a swipe sends the ring along its path with a trail and an arrow; typing, Enter, Back and the rest write their name where the last ring was. The look follows [UI-TARS-desktop](https://github.com/bytedance/UI-TARS-desktop)'s ScreenMarker, redrawn for Android. None of it is ever in a screenshot, so the model never sees it.

## Fixed

- **The capsule could catch the hands' own taps.** The model cannot see what is under the capsule, so a tap it asked for could land on *Stop* and end the run. The capsule now lets every injected gesture through, and steps to the other end of the screen when the target is under it.
- **App-icon quick actions** (long-press the icon → New chat / Voice / Camera) opened the OpenMinis chat screen instead of the home; they now open the draft inside the home, with the microphone or the camera coming up as before.

## Changed

- versionCode 16; installs over 0.1.14 without losing data.

## Install

Download `nanoMuse-0.1.15-arm64.apk` (Android 8.0 or newer, arm64) and install it over the previous version — same signing key, your data is kept. Verify: `sha256sum -c nanoMuse-0.1.15-arm64.apk.sha256`.

「动手」的过程现在看得见了，胶囊也不再挡它的路。

### 新增

- **舞台。** 它操作屏幕时，屏幕四边有一圈呼吸的光——干活时是蓝色，等你时是琥珀色。模型要点的位置上，先出现一个转动的红色虚线圆环，中心一个点，旁边写着这一步要做什么——「点击『查询』」「长按」「双击」——停一瞬再落指，落下时荡开一圈波纹。长按时第二个圆环随按住的时间填满；滑动时圆环沿路径滑过去，带着拖尾和箭头；输入、回车、返回这些没有位置的动作，把名字写在上一个圆环的位置。样式参考 [UI-TARS-desktop](https://github.com/bytedance/UI-TARS-desktop) 的 ScreenMarker，在 Android 上重画。截图时全部隐去，模型永远看不到它们。

### 修复

- **胶囊可能接住它自己的点击。** 模型看不到胶囊底下有什么，所以它要点的位置可能落在「停止」上，把任务停掉。现在每一次注入的手势都会从胶囊穿过去；目标在胶囊下面时，胶囊先挪到屏幕另一端。
- **App 图标长按的快捷方式**（新对话 / 语音 / 相机）之前打开的是 OpenMinis 的聊天页，现在在主页里打开新对话，麦克风或相机照旧弹出。

### 变更

- versionCode 16；覆盖安装 0.1.14 不丢数据。

### 安装

下载 `nanoMuse-0.1.15-arm64.apk`（Android 8.0+，arm64），覆盖安装，同一把签名，数据保留；校验：`sha256sum -c nanoMuse-0.1.15-arm64.apk.sha256`。
