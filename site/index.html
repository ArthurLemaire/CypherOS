An open-source personal AI agent. 一个开源的个人 AI 智能体。

Tell nanoMuse what you need. It searches, browses, writes, taps through the apps on your phone, works your command-line tools and MCP servers, and asks before anything it cannot undo. It runs on your own machine, with any model — including the ones that work in China. 告诉 nanoMuse 你要什么。它会搜索、浏览、写文件，操作你手机上的 App，调用你的命令行工具和 MCP 服务，遇到无法撤销的操作先问你。它跑在你自己的机器上，模型随你选——包括国内直接可用的那些。

Sixty-six seconds: one task from “ask” to “done”, then the hands, the screen, the screens, the models, the plan. No sound. 66 秒：一个任务从「说一句」到「完成」，然后是它的手、手机屏幕、五个页面、模型和计划。无声。

Ask说一句

Type it or say it, the way you would text a friend who happens to be very good with computers.像给朋友发消息一样说一句就行——一个恰好很懂电脑的朋友。

It works它去干活

In the background, on your machine. Close the app; it keeps going and tells you in the Feed when it is done.在你的机器上后台执行。关掉 App 也不停，做完写进 Feed 告诉你。

It asks first关键处先问你

A separate Sentinel checks every action. Paying, sending, deleting — you see exactly what will run, and you Allow or Deny.独立的 Sentinel 检查每一步。付款、发送、删除——先给你看将要执行什么，由你放行或拒绝。

One agent, many hands一个智能体，很多只手

The phone screen is one way to get things done, not the only one. nanoMuse picks whichever hand fits the job and mixes them in a single task. 操作手机屏幕只是其中一种方式。nanoMuse 会挑合适的那只手，也会在同一个任务里换着用。

Yours, on your machine你的，跑在你的机器上

The model proposes; the Sentinel decides. It is a separate layer with its own rules, and the model never sees your secrets. 模型提议，Sentinel 决定。它是独立的一层，有自己的规则；模型永远看不到你的密钥。

  • Allow, ask, deny放行、询问、拒绝 — per tool, per target, per domain. Grants for once, this task, a session, 24 hours, or always.——按工具、按目标、按域名。授权可以只这一次、这个任务、这次会话、24 小时或永久。
  • Vault保险箱 — keys and passwords are {{vault:NAME}} in configs and commands, resolved outside the model.——配置和命令里只写 {{vault:NAME}}，真正的值在模型之外填入。
  • Sandbox沙箱 — commands run in bubblewrap with the folders you share and nothing else.——命令跑在 bubblewrap 里，只带你共享的目录。
  • Trail留痕 — every action lands in an audit log you can read, and the Feed tells you in plain words.——每一步写入你能读的审计日志，Feed 用大白话告诉你。

Any model模型随你选

Anything with an OpenAI-compatible endpoint: DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi, GLM, MiniMax, OpenAI, or Ollama on your own machine. Domestic endpoints need no proxy. 任何 OpenAI 兼容接口都行：DeepSeek、通义千问、Kimi、GLM、MiniMax、OpenAI，或者本机的 Ollama。国内接口不需要代理。 

[llm]
provider = "openai"
model    = "deepseek-flash"
base_url = "https://api.deepseek.com"
api_key  = "${DEEPSEEK_API_KEY}"

Install安装

Two minutes. The computer runs the agent; the phone is a window onto it.两分钟。电脑跑智能体，手机是它的窗口。

1 · Computer电脑

uv tool install nanomuse
export DEEPSEEK_API_KEY=sk-...
nanomuse serve --host 0.0.0.0

Prints a URL and a QR code. Open the URL in any browser for the full app.会打印一个链接和二维码。浏览器打开链接就是完整的 App。

2 · Phone手机

Download nanomuse.apk下载 nanomuse.apk, open it, tap Scan QR code and point the camera at the terminal. Same Wi-Fi, Tailscale, or your server behind TLS all work.安装后点扫描二维码对准终端。同一 Wi-Fi、Tailscale 或带 TLS 的服务器都可以。

Or Docker或者 Docker

docker run -d --name nanomuse -p 8787:8787 \
  -e DEEPSEEK_API_KEY=sk-... \
  -v nanomuse-data:/data \
  ghcr.io/nano-muse/nanomuse:latest
